Our Story

About Sip & Chill

A little milkshake stand built on a big love for cold, creamy, made-to-order drinks. Here's who we are and what we stand for.

How We Started

Sip & Chill started as a small weekend stand with one blender, a handful of flavours, and a simple goal: make the best milkshake in the neighbourhood. Word spread fast, and today we serve handcrafted shakes made fresh, to order, with real ingredients — never pre-mixed, never rushed.

Every cup is blended after you order it, so what you get is always cold, thick, and made just for you.

Our Stand

You'll find us at 51 Peulwane Street, Midrand — come say hi, grab a shake, and watch it blended fresh right in front of you.

Prefer to skip the queue? Order ahead through our menu and we'll have it ready, or send us a message on WhatsApp and we'll sort you out.
